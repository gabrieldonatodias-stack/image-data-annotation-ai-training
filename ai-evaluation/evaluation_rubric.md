# Evaluation Rubric

## Scoring

Each dimension uses a 1–5 scale.

### 5 — Excellent
Fully satisfies the criterion with no meaningful issues.

### 4 — Good
Satisfies the criterion with minor issues.

### 3 — Acceptable
Generally useful but contains noticeable limitations.

### 2 — Weak
Significant problems reduce usefulness.

### 1 — Poor
Fails to satisfy the criterion or contains major errors.

## Evaluation Rules

### Accuracy
Check whether factual claims are correct and logically supported.

### Relevance
Check whether the response directly addresses the user's request without unnecessary digression.

### Clarity
Check organization, readability, wording and ease of understanding.

### Instruction Following
Check explicit requirements such as language, format, length, constraints and requested scope.

### Completeness
Check whether important parts of the request were addressed.

### Overall Quality
Consider the response as a whole, balancing correctness, usefulness, relevance and adherence to instructions.

## Feedback Standard

Evaluator feedback should be:
- Specific
- Evidence-based
- Concise
- Actionable
- Neutral in tone

Avoid vague feedback such as "good answer" or "bad answer."
