# Annotation Guidelines

Assign the category that best represents the main visual subject.

## Taxonomy
Vehicle — transportation objects.
Animal — animals.
Person — human figures.
Food — meals, ingredients or food products.
Electronic Device — phones, laptops, tablets and similar devices.

## Confidence
0.90–1.00: very clear
0.75–0.89: clear with minor ambiguity
0.50–0.74: uncertain
Below 0.50: review required

## Quality rules
Use approved category names, unique IDs, confidence from 0 to 1, and review ambiguous samples.
